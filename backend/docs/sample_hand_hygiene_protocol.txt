Hand Hygiene Protocol (WHO 5 Moments)

Perform hand hygiene before touching a patient, before any clean or aseptic task, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after contact with the patient's surroundings. Use alcohol-based hand rub for at least 20 seconds when hands are not visibly soiled. Wash with soap and water after visible contamination or suspected spore exposure. Remove rings, watches, and bracelets before scrubbing.
