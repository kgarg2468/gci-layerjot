CLABSI Sterile Insertion Checklist

Before central line insertion:
1. Perform hand hygiene immediately before donning sterile PPE.
2. Use maximal sterile barrier precautions: cap, mask, sterile gown, sterile gloves.
3. Prep insertion site with chlorhexidine and allow full drying time.
4. Apply a full-body sterile drape, exposing only the insertion site.
5. Avoid touching non-sterile surfaces after gloving.

Maintenance reminders:
- Assess daily line necessity and remove as soon as no longer needed.
- Keep dressings clean, dry, and intact.
- Scrub the hub before access per protocol.
